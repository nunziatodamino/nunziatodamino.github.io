# Portfolio asset preparation

The originals under `paintings/` remain untouched. Publication copies are auto-oriented, resized only when larger than 2400 × 2400 pixels, stripped of camera metadata and encoded as WebP at quality 86. No generative edits, colour changes, retouching, enlargement or perspective reconstruction are applied.

## From the scrolls

The nine files in `paintings/From the scrolls/` map in filename order to `content/painter/images/from-the-scrolls/passage-01.webp` through `passage-09.webp`.

They document passages from two continuous, uncut plotter-paper rolls, each 50 m × 60 cm. It is no longer known which photographed passage belongs to which roll.
