$$
\hat{\mathscr H}_0
\varphi_{n\ell m_\ell m_s}
=
E_n
\varphi_{n\ell m_\ell m_s}.
$$

With

$$
\varphi_{n\ell m_\ell m_s}
=
\psi_{n\ell m_\ell}
\chi_{\frac12,m_s}.
$$

Where we recall

$$
E_n
=
-\frac{E_0}{n^2},
\qquad
E_0=13.6\,\mathrm{eV}.
$$

The quantum numbers are

$$
n=1,2,\ldots
\qquad
\text{principal quantum number},
$$

$$
\ell=0,1,\ldots,n-1
\qquad
\text{orbital quantum number},
$$

$$
m_\ell=-\ell,-\ell+1,\ldots,\ell
\qquad
(2\ell+1)
$$

$$
s=\frac12,
$$

$$
m_s=-\frac12,+\frac12.
$$

$E_n$ is degenerate, in the sense that for fixed $n$ there are

$$
2\sum_{\ell=0}^{n-1}(2\ell+1)
=
2n^2
$$

states with the same $E_n$.
