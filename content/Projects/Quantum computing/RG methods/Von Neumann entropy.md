Consider a state described by a density operator.
We define Von Neumann entropy:

$$ S = - \mbox{Tr}(\rho \log_2 \rho) $$

Considering the representation of the density operator, if we consider its eigenvalues $\lambda_i$, then the Von Neumann entropy can be evaluate easily as :

$$ S = - \sum_i \lambda_i \log_2 \lambda_i $$
