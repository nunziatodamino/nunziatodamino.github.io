Apply minimal coupling,

$$  
\hat{\bar p}\longrightarrow\hat{\bar\pi}  
=\hat{\bar p}-q\bar A,  
\qquad  
\hat{\mathscr H}\longrightarrow\hat{\mathscr H}-q\Phi.  
$$

Then

$$  
\boxed{  
i\hbar\partial_t\Psi  
=\left[  
c\hat{\bar\alpha}\cdot(\hat{\bar p}-q\bar A)  
+\hat\beta mc^2  
+q\Phi\mathbb 1_4  
\right]\Psi  
}.  
$$
