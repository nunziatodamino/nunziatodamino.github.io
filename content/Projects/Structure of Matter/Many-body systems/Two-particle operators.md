In the context of a N-particle system, we define a two particle operator

$$  
\hat V|\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_N\rangle  
=\frac12\sum_{i\neq j}\hat V_{ij}|\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_N\rangle,  
$$
Where:

$$  
\hat V_{ij}  
=\mathbb{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\hat V_{ij}^{(2)}\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathbb{1}  
$$

Acts jointly on the i-th and j-th space.
With the same ansatz as before:

$$  
\langle\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_N|  
\hat V|\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_N\rangle  
=\frac12\sum_{i\neq j}  
\left(  
\prod_{k\neq i,j}\langle\alpha_k|\beta_k\rangle  
\right)  
\langle\alpha_i\alpha_j|\hat V_{ij}|\beta_i\beta_j\rangle.  
$$
