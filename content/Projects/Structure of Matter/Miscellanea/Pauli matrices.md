The Pauli matrices are defined as:

$$
\sigma_1 \equiv \sigma_x \equiv X = \begin{pmatrix} 0 &1 \\1&0\end{pmatrix}, \quad
\sigma_2 \equiv \sigma_y \equiv Y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 &-i \\i&0\end{pmatrix}, \quad
\sigma_3 \equiv \sigma_z \equiv Z = \begin{pmatrix} 1 &0 \\0&-1\end{pmatrix} \quad
$$

### Properties

-  $$ \sigma_i\sigma_j = \delta_{ij}\mathbb{1} + i \varepsilon_{ijk} \sigma_k$$
- $$ [\sigma_i, \sigma_j] = 2i \varepsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k $$

Where $\varepsilon_{ijk}$ is the [[Levi-Civita symbol]].

-  $$ \sigma_i^2 = \mathbb{1}_2 $$

- $$(\bar\sigma\cdot\bar a)(\bar\sigma\cdot\bar b) =\bar a\cdot\bar b+i\bar\sigma\cdot(\bar a\times\bar b)$$
